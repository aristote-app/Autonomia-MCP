# Inbound acquisition architecture

Autonomia is the commercial system of record for all inbound acquisition generated by the future website, landing pages, Meta campaigns and other lead sources.

## Separation of responsibilities

- **Website / landing-page project**: owns UX, SEO, forms, landing pages and conversion experience.
- **Communication / acquisition project**: owns messages, creatives, Meta campaigns, campaign structure and media performance.
- **Autonomia**: receives the lead, preserves attribution, deduplicates, enriches, qualifies, assigns, creates the opportunity, proposes the next action and measures downstream business value.

All acquisition surfaces must send the same normalized lead contract to Autonomia.

## Supported inbound sources

Initial source families:
- website forms
- landing-page forms
- Meta Lead Ads
- inbound email
- chat / conversational form
- referral / partner
- webinar / event
- manually imported inbound lead

Future sources may use the same contract.

## Normalized inbound lead contract

### Identity
- external_lead_id
- source_channel
- source_platform
- received_at
- first_name
- last_name
- email
- phone
- job_title
- company_name
- company_domain
- company_siren / siret when available

### Expressed intent
- requested_service
- message
- landing_page_topic
- estimated_budget when explicitly supplied
- desired_timeline when explicitly supplied
- company_size when supplied
- preferred_contact_channel when supplied

### Attribution
- landing_page_url
- referrer_url
- form_id
- campaign_id / campaign_name
- adset_id / adset_name
- ad_id / ad_name
- creative_id / creative_name
- utm_source
- utm_medium
- utm_campaign
- utm_content
- utm_term
- click identifiers when legitimately available

Never overwrite original first-touch data. Additional touchpoints must be appended to attribution history.

### Consent / privacy evidence
Store the consent facts received from the acquisition surface:
- marketing_consent value
- consent_timestamp
- privacy_notice_version
- consent_source
- submitted_ip only if the implemented privacy policy and legal design require it

Autonomia must preserve the evidence supplied by the source rather than infer consent.

## Automatic inbound workflow

1. Receive and validate payload.
2. Store immutable raw source evidence.
3. Spam / malformed-submission checks.
4. Deduplicate person, company and lead.
5. Link or create organization.
6. Enrich the organization from permitted sources.
7. Classify the probable need:
   - AI consulting / transformation
   - GenAI / LLM / RAG / agents
   - automation
   - governance / AI Act
   - training
   - staffing / freelance
   - public-sector need
   - partnership
   - other
8. Create or update the canonical opportunity.
9. Calculate:
   - Autonomia fit
   - intent strength
   - urgency
   - completeness of evidence
   - economic potential when evidence exists
10. Determine the next best action.
11. Assign an owner or team according to routing rules.
12. Notify only when human attention is useful.
13. Draft the first response / call brief if appropriate.
14. Track the lead through meeting, proposal, won/lost and revenue.
15. Feed the result back into acquisition analytics.

## Opportunity orchestration

An inbound lead does not remain a simple contact. It can create or enrich:
- a private B2B opportunity
- a training opportunity
- a staffing opportunity
- a partnership opportunity
- a cross-sell opportunity

One inbound lead may create several linked commercial opportunities if evidence supports them.

## Multi-user workflow

Every inbound lead/opportunity can have:
- owner
- contributors
- team
- status
- next action
- due date
- comments
- tasks
- activity history
- audit log

Routing examples:
- training inquiry -> Training team
- enterprise AI transformation -> Consulting / Direction
- freelance requirement -> Staffing
- public-sector inquiry -> Public Markets

## Lead lifecycle

Suggested states:
- new
- enriched
- qualified
- needs_review
- contacted
- meeting
- proposal
- negotiation
- won
- lost
- disqualified

Status changes must be auditable.

## Attribution and business analytics

Autonomia should measure beyond form submissions.

### Acquisition funnel
- visitors when analytics data is connected
- form starts
- leads
- qualified leads
- meetings
- proposals
- won opportunities
- revenue
- gross margin when available

### Breakdowns
- channel
- campaign
- landing page
- ad set
- ad
- creative
- offer
- audience when provided by the advertising platform
- period
- owner

When ad-spend APIs are connected, compute:
- CPL

- cost per meeting

- CAC
- revenue / spend
- margin / spend

Never invent missing advertising spend or attribution data.

## Meta Lead Ads

Meta lead forms should eventually connect through the official webhook / Graph API flow.

The Meta connector should preserve:
- Meta lead ID
- form ID
- campaign ID
- ad set ID
- ad ID
- creative context when exposed
- submission timestamp
- submitted fields

Then it sends the normalized contract into the same Autonomia inbound pipeline used by website and LP forms.

## Landing pages and pre-filled campaigns

Landing pages may be campaign-specific and forms may be pre-filled where the platform and user consent allow it.

The LP must pass campaign context invisibly with the lead so that Autonomia knows:
- what the prospect saw
- which offer was presented
- which campaign/ad generated the visit
- what CTA was used
- what the prospect submitted

## Next-best-action engine

Examples:
- strong training inquiry -> prepare program + propose meeting
- strong staffing need -> search consultant pool immediately
- strategic enterprise signal -> identify likely decision-maker and create account brief
- incomplete but high-fit lead -> request the missing qualification data
- duplicate lead -> merge with existing account and show prior history

The system should avoid noisy notifications. The principal human surface is **À traiter aujourd'hui**.

## Learning loop

For each inbound source and campaign, Autonomia should learn from downstream outcomes:
- qualification
- meeting obtained
- proposal sent
- won/lost
- revenue
- margin
- sales-cycle length
- reasons for loss / disqualification when recorded

These outcomes improve routing and prioritization. They do not convert correlation into unsupported causal claims.

## Shared contract for future chats

### Website chat
Must implement forms and LPs against this lead contract and preserve attribution.

### Communication chat
Must design campaigns, offers, creatives and Meta structure with stable campaign/ad identifiers so Autonomia can measure downstream results.

### Autonomia chat
Owns ingestion, CRM/orchestration, enrichment, opportunity creation, scoring, assignment, next-best-action, analytics and learning.
