# Editorial priority — Wave 1

This document records why selected topics are promoted from the 400-topic backlog. It does not invent monthly search volume.

## Evidence model

A topic may receive priority when several independent signals align:

- current SERP presence for a commercial or educational query;
- official vendor training/documentation around the use case;
- recent French job-market wording showing companies staffing the capability;
- direct commercial fit with Autonomia Experts or Autonomia Academy;
- ability to create genuinely distinct, technically useful long-form content.

## Windsor rule

Windsor / Search Console / Google Ads data, when available to the active ChatGPT session, is used to confirm or reprioritize queries. Employment demand and SERP observation remain separate evidence types. A hiring term is not treated as search volume.
